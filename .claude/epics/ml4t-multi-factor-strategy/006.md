## Problem Statement
Static factor weights fail to adapt to changing market conditions in Taiwan markets. A dynamic allocation system is needed that adjusts factor weights based on regime detection to maximize risk-adjusted returns while maintaining diversification and risk control.

## Technical Requirements

### Core Implementation
1. **Regime-Specific Weight Matrices**
   - **Trending Regime**: Momentum (40%), Growth (25%), Quality (20%), Value (10%), Size (5%)
   - **Mean-Reverting Regime**: Value (35%), Quality (30%), Size (20%), Growth (10%), Momentum (5%)
   - **Choppy Regime**: Quality (40%), Low Vol (30%), Size (15%), Value (10%), Growth (5%)

2. **Transition Smoothing**
   - Implement exponential smoothing during regime transitions
   - Prevent sudden weight shifts that cause excessive turnover
   - Use confidence scores to modulate transition speed
   - Maximum 5% daily weight change per factor

3. **Risk Control Framework**
   - Maintain total leverage constraints (≤130%)
   - Implement factor concentration limits (max 50% single factor)
   - Track ex-ante volatility and adjust for risk parity
   - Include drawdown controls and volatility targeting

### Taiwan Market Adaptations
- Account for Taiwan sector biases (Technology weight limits)
- Consider liquidity constraints for small-cap allocations
- Implement currency hedging weight adjustments
- Factor in Taiwan-specific risk factors (political, trade dependencies)
