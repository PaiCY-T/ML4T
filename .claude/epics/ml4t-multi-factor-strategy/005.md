## Problem Statement
Current multi-factor strategies fail to adapt to changing market conditions in Taiwan markets. A robust regime detection system is needed to identify when the market is trending (momentum-favoring), mean-reverting (reversal-favoring), or choppy (low-signal environment), enabling dynamic factor allocation.

## Technical Requirements

### Core Implementation
1. **TAIEX vs MA200 Analysis**
   - Calculate 200-day moving average for TAIEX
   - Implement price position relative to MA200
   - Track duration above/below MA200
   - Calculate slope and volatility of MA200

2. **Regime Classification Logic**
   - **Trending Regime**: TAIEX > MA200 for >20 days, MA200 slope >0.05%/day
   - **Mean-Reverting Regime**: TAIEX oscillating around MA200, volatility <historical 25th percentile
   - **Choppy Regime**: High volatility, frequent MA200 crossovers, inconclusive trends

3. **Confidence Scoring System**
   - Score 0-100 for regime classification certainty
   - Factor in: trend strength, volatility persistence, momentum consistency
   - Implement smoothing to prevent excessive regime switching

### Taiwan Market Specifics
- Use TAIEX as primary market index
- Account for Taiwan trading holidays and sessions
- Consider currency stability (TWD/USD) as regime modifier
- Include sector rotation analysis (Tech/Financial/Traditional)
