   - **Sideways Market Optimization**: Mean-reversion and low-volatility factors

   - Real-time factor effectiveness monitoring
   - Adaptive factor inclusion/exclusion criteria
   - Performance-based factor weight adjustments
   - Statistical significance testing for factor contributions

1. **Regime Detection Parameters**
   - Volatility threshold optimization (current: 0.02 daily)

   - Confidence thresholds for regime classification

2. **Portfolio Construction Parameters**
   - Position sizing optimization (max 2% per stock)
   - Rebalancing frequency optimization (daily vs weekly)
   - Transaction cost threshold optimization
   - Risk budget allocation across factors

3. **Optimization Framework**
   - Bayesian optimization for hyperparameter search
   - Cross-validation across multiple time periods
   - Walk-forward optimization methodology
   - Out-of-sample validation protocols

   - Market impact models for large orders

2. **Cost-Aware Trading Logic**
   - Minimum trade size thresholds
   - Partial rebalancing strategies
   - Trade bundling and netting optimization
   - Timing optimization for market open/close

3. **Liquidity Management**
   - ADV (Average Daily Volume) constraints
   - Market cap and free float requirements
   - Trading hour optimization strategies
   - Queue position estimation models

   - Memory usage optimization for large datasets

   - Database query optimization
